# V23: recover the original question, not every old mechanism

Ryō requested a revival of the earliest Latent Workspace intent while separating
question encoding, reading, exact copying and preservation of ordinary host
ability. This is a design synthesis; it does not dispatch training or claim 1.0.

The [completed W/Q/R diagnosis](../../provenance/pilots/v23_state_factorial01_20261021/README.md)
now grounds the [next engineering sequence](NEXT_FACTORS.md). It distinguishes
query-sensitive early identity errors from a writer–upper-reader late-copy
interaction, and also exposes how much exact-token gain is output formatting.
These findings strengthen the need for four distinct endpoints below.

## Four distinct promises

The repository's [historical reconstruction](../v20/SCOPE.md) and live promoted
[lattice](../../src/latent_workspace/lattice.py) /
[loss](../../src/latent_workspace/losses.py) preserve the v4/v5 idea: a trajectory
formed from the model's own states, recurrent processing and causal broadcast,
with recoverable information and non-collapsed geometry. The later deferred
memory flip test made content-specific availability identifiable, but it was
never a complete definition of the workspace.

| Promise | What V22 establishes on its exposed panel | What remains to test |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Availability | Independently encoded content reaches native answers; matched-length twin edits matter | Question-to-fact binding, unaffected-answer stability, exact identity transport on fresh worlds |
| Occupancy | Cut-4 states can be saved and read without their raw passage in the query | Reusable cross-query/multi-turn state, persistence and selective overwrite with controls |
| Deliberation | Upper native layers compute over packed memory and query | Benefit specifically from additional latent computation: matched-budget depth/step controls and bypass; native depth alone is not proof |
| Consequence | Full updates improve this memory panel but alter ordinary outputs | Independent base floors, reasoning consistency, writing/code fidelity and complete paired generations |

V7's carrier-necessity result was not semantic necessity; its unchanged donor
choices must not be blended with V22's content-use result. V19.5's lattice gain
without step dependence likewise remains a readout result, not latent thought.
The [architecture contract](../ARCHITECTURE.md) keeps the host binding separate
from the algorithm, with one live engine and immutable historical sources.

## V23 working hypothesis

The desired milestone is **state formation → question-conditioned recruitment
→ useful native computation → faithful output**, while ordinary no-memory
capability survives. Exact copying is necessary information transport, not the
entire objective; a binary flip is a useful causal test, not the whole value.

First use [W/Q/R state factorization](STATE_FACTORIAL_01.md) to localize the
existing gain and harmful interactions. Do not add another similarly named
contrastive loss before checking where its gradient would act. Keep the full
native vocabulary, compositional cut interface, shared complete-owner state
and exact restart. Detached state banks are diagnostic only, never a replacement
for the training graph's re-encoding and gradients.

Then freeze independent learner factors rather than bundling a recipe:

1. **Recruitment / binding:** train-only multi-query worlds, with the same
   memory and different questions, local edits and unrelated-question controls.
   Inspect query-state versus upper-reader gradients and their conflicts.
2. **Identity / copy:** train-only varied symbols and shared-prefix entities,
   later token supervision and exact identity span diagnostics. Do not train
   on the exposed V22 errors or use their patch masks as acceptance targets.
3. **Host preservation:** a separately frozen original-host logit anchor on
   the train-only preservation bank, alongside ordinary CE. Measure paired
   losses/repairs on untouched confirmation tasks; freezing the head alone
   does not protect changed head inputs.
4. **Deliberation revival:** only after transport and preservation are sound,
   compare a shared/native computation step or feedback path with matched
   compute/capacity controls and an inference bypass. Reintroducing a lattice,
   auxiliary geometry loss and several other changes at once is not that test.

These are hypotheses. A learned recurrent adapter is not automatically the
final architecture: the agreed destination is native full FT. Extra latent
steps, KV persistence and multi-turn quality each need their own causal and
resource qualifications, not evidence borrowed from the present factorial.

Keep geometry/retention diagnostics as health measures, not substitutes for
answers. Keep abstention, exact copy, relational reasoning, free writing and
base retention as separate outcomes. Freeze a fresh confirmation panel and
acceptance thresholds before V23 weight selection; retain **FAIL / winner:none /
non_regression NOT_ESTABLISHED** until those gates actually pass.
